# Sample Daily Morning Brief — AINN style

Format: South Africa first → Key facts → Why it matters → AINN watch → Editor’s take

Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
By: [Reporter Name] — Edited by: [Editor Name]

1) South Africa-first (top SA item)
- Headline: [Clear, one-line headline — SA first]
- Key facts: 3 bullet points with sourced facts (links to primary statements or docs).
- Why it matters: 1–2 sentences explaining local impact.

2) Other SA items (2 short bullets)
- [Headline 1] — 1-line summary + link.
- [Headline 2] — 1-line summary + link.

3) Global items (top 5)
- [1] Model release / funding / regulation item — 1-line summary + link.
- [2] Conference announcement or paper — link.
- [3] Product launch — link.
- [4] Research highlight — link.
- [5] Industry move — link.

AINN watch
- 2–3 items to watch in the next 48–72 hours (what could change / what we are tracking).

Editor’s take (optional, 1 short paragraph)
- Small, signed commentary from an editor placing the top SA story in context and next steps readers should expect.

Notes
- Every claim must link to a primary source if possible. If relying on a secondary report, label it and state attempts to contact primary sources.
- For embargoed information, include embargo metadata and editor approval line.
