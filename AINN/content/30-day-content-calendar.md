# 30-day content calendar — 14 Aug–12 Sep 2026

This calendar is a working editorial schedule for AINN’s launch month (South Africa-first). It balances daily briefs with weekly features across vertical beats and includes conference verification rules.

Calendar rules
- Daily Morning Briefs: publish every weekday morning (Mon–Fri) with 3 South Africa-first items + 5 global items.
- Weekly feature: publish one deep-dive per week (Monday or Tuesday).
- Conference verification rule: verify events from at least two authoritative sources (organiser website + official social account or partner site). Do not publish schedule/dates based solely on third-party conference listing sites without organiser confirmation.

Weekly themes
- Week 1 (14–20 Aug): Policy & regulation; launch briefing; university research highlight; startup funding.
- Week 2 (21–27 Aug): Healthcare pilots; reproducibility notebook launch; enterprise adoption case study.
- Week 3 (28 Aug–3 Sep): Agriculture & mining applications; data infrastructure and skills.
- Week 4 (4–10 Sep): Responsible AI; cybersecurity and model safety; Pan-African collaborations.
- Final days (11–12 Sep): Conference preview + post-event coverage plan.

Daily cadence (example weekdays)
- 06:30: News desk final check / incoming tips.
- 07:00: Publish Daily Morning Brief (3 SA-first, 5 global, AINN watch, editor’s take).
- 09:00–12:00: Reporting windows (interviews, data checks, reproducibility runs).
- 13:00: Afternoon roundup (updates to published pieces; live blog updates if required).
- 16:00: Editorial review for next-day brief and story assignments.

Editorial assignments (rotating)
- Monday: Policy & Regulation deep-dive.
- Tuesday: Research & Reproducibility feature.
- Wednesday: Vertical (Healthcare / Agriculture / Mining) case study.
- Thursday: Product/Model review or enterprise pilot.
- Friday: Weekend-read feature or long-form interview / podcast highlight.

Nightly watch
- News desk monitors social feeds, press offices, and university announcement lists for breaking items.
- If a breaking SA-first item is identified, follow the emergency verification checklist and escalate to AI News Editor.

KPIs for the month
- Publish 20 Daily Briefs (weekday coverage).
- Publish 4 in-depth features (weekly).
- Publish 1 reproducibility notebook and dataset.
- Achieve first 500 newsletter subscribers (target for launch month).
