# AINews — South Africa-first AI news organisation

This repository contains the editorial foundation for AINews: a professional AI news organisation launching with a South Africa focus and expanding regionally and globally.

Purpose

- Seed the newsroom with a clear mission, editorial standards, and reusable templates so writers, editors, and contributors can quickly publish accurate, verifiable, and impactful AI journalism.

What’s in this repo

- README.md — mission, launch summary, contribution notes (this file).
- docs/ORG-CHART.md — newsroom organisational chart and role descriptions.
- docs/ARTICLE-TEMPLATES.md — article templates, publishing checklists, reproducibility notes, and editorial workflows.

Quick start

1. Read the org chart (docs/ORG-CHART.md) to understand roles and responsibilities.
2. Use the article templates (docs/ARTICLE-TEMPLATES.md) as scaffolds when drafting.
3. Run the verification checklist in every story: link primary sources, reproduce claims where feasible, flag sensitive/high-risk items for legal/ethics review.

Editorial policy highlights

- Human sign-off required: AI may assist drafting, monitoring, and summarising, but a named editor must verify and sign off on every published story.
- Sources: all factual claims must cite primary sources (papers, datasets, official statements). Whenever possible, link to the exact paper, DOI, preprint, code repo, or dataset.
- Corrections: publish clear correction notes with date and brief explanation; keep an edit log in article metadata.
- Harm minimisation: do not publish content that meaningfully facilitates illegal wrongdoing, clinical harm, or other high-risk activities.

Launch plan (first month — South Africa)

- Week 0 (prep): populate contact lists for universities, startups, government; set embargo rules; set up reproducible notebooks repo.
- Week 1: publish a Daily Morning Brief, 3 local pieces (policy, a university research highlight, startup funding/launch).
- Week 2: reproducibility article with a public notebook; a local enterprise pilot case study.
- Week 3: feature/investigation (AI in mining or health), and a video explainer.
- Week 4: launch weekly newsletter and first podcast.

How to contribute

- Create issues for proposed stories or beats.
- Use PRs to add or update templates and workflows.
- Tag editors and leads in PRs for review and sign-off.
