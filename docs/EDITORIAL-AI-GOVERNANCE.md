# Editorial & AI Governance Policy

## 1. Core rule

AINews is a human-led newsroom. AI may assist discovery, research, transcription, drafting, translation, classification and analysis. **AI may not independently publish, correct, retract or materially alter a published story.**

## 2. Source hierarchy

For factual claims, prefer sources in this order:

1. Primary documents and datasets.
2. Official government, regulator, league, club, company or institutional statements.
3. Peer-reviewed research and authoritative technical reports.
4. Direct interviews and on-the-record expert evidence.
5. Reputable secondary reporting when primary evidence is unavailable.

AI-generated summaries are not sources.

## 3. AI output controls

Every AI-assisted story must record, internally:

- model/provider;
- model/version where available;
- task performed;
- date/time of generation;
- source material supplied to the model;
- material edits made by a human;
- final editor.

Do not paste confidential source material, personal data, unpublished medical information, credentials or secrets into external AI systems unless the approved data-processing arrangement explicitly permits it.

## 4. High-risk coverage

Health, safety, crime, elections, financial markets, allegations against identifiable people, minors and investigative stories require enhanced human review. AI output must never be treated as sufficient evidence for a high-risk claim.

## 5. AI disclosure

If AI materially contributes to a published story, the publication system should retain an internal AI-use record. Public disclosure should be used when the AI contribution materially affects the reader's understanding, such as automated translation, synthetic media or generated visualisations.

## 6. Corrections

Corrections must preserve the original audit trail. A correction record should contain:

- article ID;
- correction timestamp;
- original claim;
- corrected claim;
- reason;
- source supporting the correction;
- editor approving the change.

## 7. Sports-specific rule

Transfer rumours, injuries, suspensions, line-ups and match statistics must be independently verified against an authoritative source or approved data provider. AI aggregation alone is never sufficient.

## 8. Publication gate

A story is publishable only when:

- factual claims have been checked;
- source links/evidence are attached;
- conflicts of interest are declared where relevant;
- required specialist review is complete;
- a named editor signs off;
- the audit trail is complete.
