# Article Templates & Publishing Checklist

This document contains reusable templates and checklists to speed up reporting and ensure consistent quality.

Publishing checklist (required for every story)

- [ ] Primary sources linked (papers, press releases, datasets, statements).
- [ ] At least one named editor has reviewed and signed off.
- [ ] Reproducibility notes included when a claim depends on data or experiments.
- [ ] Legal/Ethics review completed for high-risk content.
- [ ] Images and media have rights cleared.
- [ ] Tags added (model-name, dataset, SouthAfrica, regulation, reproducibility).

Story templates

1) Breaking News (short)

- Headline:
- Lede (1-2 sentences): what happened and why it matters.
- What (details): who, what, when, where.
- Evidence & sources: links to statements/papers/repositories.
- Significance: expert quote or short analysis.
- Next steps / watch list.
- Byline & editor name.

2) Research Report

- Headline:
- Summary (3-4 sentences): main claim, context, and significance.
- Background: prior work and why this matters.
- Methods: dataset, model architecture, metrics, reproducibility notes.
- Results: key numbers, visualizations, replicability assessment.
- Expert commentary: external reactions or peer-review notes.
- Limitations & conflicts of interest.
- Sources & reproducibility repo link.

3) Analysis / Feature

- Headline:
- Nut graf: what the piece will show and why readers should care.
- Narrative: structured sections with evidence, interviews, and data.
- Visuals and sidebars: explainers, methodology, timeline.
- Conclusion & implications.
- Sources, credits, and byline.

4) Product / Model Review

- Headline:
- Short summary: what is being reviewed and overall verdict.
- Methodology: datasets, evaluation metrics, hardware used.
- Results: performance, failure modes, safety concerns.
- Recommendation: who should use it, risks, and alternatives.
- Reproducible artifacts: scripts, notebooks, data used.

Reproducibility notes

- Publish notebooks for data-driven pieces at /reproducible under an open license.
- Document exact versions of models, datasets, and evaluation code.
- Provide instructions for running experiments and visualisations.

Labels and taxonomy

- Label content as News / Analysis / Opinion / Investigation.
- Use tags: model-name, dataset, startup, regulation, SouthAfrica, reproducibility.

Embargo & source handling

- Maintain an embargo tracker file in the repo for embargoed briefings.
- Respect source confidentiality; use anonymous sources only when necessary and with editor approval.

Style & tone

- Clear, factual, and accessible. Use technical detail where needed but explain jargon.
- Distinguish clearly between verification, speculation, and opinion.
