# AINews — Organisational Chart and Roles

Executive Leadership

- Editor-in-Chief
  - Sets editorial vision, approves major investigations, manages crises, builds relationships with institutions and regulators.

- Managing Editor
  - Runs day-to-day newsroom operations, assigns stories, manages deadlines, coordinates editors and reporters.

Editorial Department

- AI News Editor
  - Oversees breaking news: major model releases, corporate announcements, regulatory developments. Verifies sources and manages embargoes.

- Research & Science Editor
  - Ensures research stories accurately reflect methods and results, coordinates peer review for high-stakes pieces.

- Policy & Regulation Editor
  - Leads coverage of government strategy, legislation, procurement, and regulatory developments.

- Ethics & Investigations Editor
  - Oversees investigations into safety, bias, misuse, and societal impacts. Coordinates legal and ethical review.

Reporting Department

- Regional Correspondents (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Pretoria, Durban)
  - Local reporting on universities, startups, enterprise pilots, and regional policy.

- Startups & Funding Reporter
  - Tracks funding rounds, exits, product launches, and company growth.

- Research Correspondent / Science Reporter
  - Covers papers, conferences, and university labs.

- Product & Model Reviewer
  - Tests and reviews models, tools, and products; documents methodology and limitations.

- Data Journalist
  - Produces data-driven stories, dashboards, and reproducible analyses.

Vertical Beats

- Healthcare Reporter
- Agriculture Reporter
- Mining Reporter
- Finance Reporter

Multimedia

- Video Producer
- Podcast Producer
- Photographer / Videographer
- Graphic Designer

Digital & Community

- Social Media Manager
- Website Editor
- Newsletter Editor
- Audience Engagement Editor

Research & Verification

- AI Research Assistant
  - Monitor papers, preprints, patents, and announcements; prepare summaries for editors.

- AI Data Analyst / Reproducibility Engineer
  - Reproduce results, maintain benchmark suites, publish notebooks.

- Statistician

Operations & Commercial

- Partnerships Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Legal Counsel / Privacy Officer

Notes

- Start small: one person may hold multiple roles in launch. Clearly label owners for each beat.
- Maintain a contacts database with PR/press, academic liaisons, and regulator contacts.
